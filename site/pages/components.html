What's in it

Eleven pieces. Take any.

Each one has its own repository, its own tests and its own package. They share a look and a set of libraries when they're built, and nothing when they run except what you chose to install.

§2Underneath

36 libraries, on crates.io.

The pieces stand on a stack of small libraries, all published and all usable without the rest. Several have nothing to do with Hyprland: the freedesktop MIME database, icon theme lookup, the thumbnail cache everyone shares, zip, tar and 7z, and a way to wait for another program that gives up eventually.
